HEIC/HEIF photo conversion

This application uses heic-to 1.6.7 by hoppergee, licensed under the
GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 or later (LGPL-3.0-or-later).
It includes libheif 1.23.7 and libde265 1.0.16 (LGPL).
The library is loaded separately, only for local HEIC/HEIF conversion.
No camera originals are sent to the library author or a conversion service.

in its README. The separately loaded browser worker can be replaced with
